# ⚠ The loud direction is DOWN the ladder `Bare` → `Seeded` → `-`: the sheet still declares the
# rule and no control builds anything under the scope it names any more, or no longer without its

# control that only exists inside another, is unreached by both sweeps.

# kept. A `-` row the editor's census does not credit either is named, with what builds it, in
