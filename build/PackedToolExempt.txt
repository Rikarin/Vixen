# Ultz.Native.Assimp 6.0.2 ships two sonames in every Linux and macOS RID and Silk.NET.Assimp
# 2.23.0's AssimpLibraryNameContainer opens exactly one of them — libassimp.so.5 on Linux,
# libassimp.5.dylib on macOS. Vixen.Sdk.csproj drops the 6 by name, 44,251,540 bytes across the five
# RIDs that carry a pair, and says in as many words that globbing the version would silently drop
# both the day the binding moves to 6. The major 5 IS carried and is what a model importer opens; if
# the binding ever moves, these five lines are what has to be deleted rather than edited.
